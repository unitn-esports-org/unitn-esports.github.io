Who we are

UniTn E-sports is a student sports association founded in 2022 with the aim of creating a community of students who can share their passion for competitive video games.
The association aims to create student teams that participate in tournaments and competitions, representing the University of Trento in the field of E-sports.
UniTn E-sports organizes both online and offline events and competitions, involving students from different faculties and academic years, and aims to develop an external network of relationships with other universities.

Offline and online events

Regularly scheduled offline and online events, both competitive and recreational, to foster a sense of community.
Check out our social channels or the calendar in the site to stay up to date with the upcoming events!

Community building

We are committed to building a welcoming and inclusive community for all students, regardless if they are interested or not in competitive gaming.
Our social channels and community spaces, like our Discord server, are always open to anyone who wants to connect with like-minded people.

Representation and networking

Outside of the university, we aim to create a network of relationships with other universities and organizations in the E-sports and gaming field, to represent the university, create opportunities for growth and, most importantly, have fun.
