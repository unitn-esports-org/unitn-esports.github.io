Whistleblowing

Report concerns securely and privately.

UniTn E-sports is committed to a safe and respectful environment.
Use this confidential channel to report harassment, discrimination, misconduct, or other concerns.
Please include as much detail as possible so an administrative member can review your report and take appropriate action.
They may request further information or another way to contact you; an in-person meeting is optional (can be requested).
Messages sent through this form are accessible to all administrators with access to the association email account.
To contact a specific member, you may also reach out through Discord or in person.
